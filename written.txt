Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
A: As the circles move the bounding boxes get larger and larger and begin to overlap more. This is due to fullRefit() because it updates
   the bounds of each node without changing which circels belong to which group. Over time BVH gets less efficient since the overlapping boxes mean 
   more intersection checks.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
A: When we use reset(), the bvh is rebuilt every frame with the new positions of the circles. This allows the bounding boxes to stay tighter and not overlap
   as much, even if it is more expensive than reffiting. 

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
A: Some circles could be drawn with their sides sliced off because BVH rejects sample points that fall outside of a node's bounding box. If a bounding box doesn't fully contain
   a circle, valid intersections could be getting skipped.
